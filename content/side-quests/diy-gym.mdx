I wanted a gym at home that made training easier to start, harder to skip, and good enough to film in. That meant it could not just be equipment in a corner. It needed to work for outdoor workouts, content, lighting angles, camera placement, movement space, and the premium feel of the gear itself. If the setup looked cheap, felt cramped, or took too much effort to reset, it would fail. The goal was simple: build a space that makes working out and creating content feel inevitable.
