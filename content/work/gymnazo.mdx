At Gymnazo, I worked across product, programming, sales, and coach development. The two biggest bodies of work were precision-based fitness programming and a coach development system that later became a standalone certification product.

## Problem

Gymnazo was not a normal gym. It operated more like a hybrid physical therapy clinic and performance institute, serving everyone from teen athletes to older adults. That created a difficult scaling problem: the coaching quality bar was high, the movement vocabulary was complex, and clients expected individualized results.

The company needed programming that could work across a wide demographic while still being reproducible by different coaches. It also needed to onboard new coaches faster without lowering the standard of instruction.

## Product Vision

The product vision was to turn complex movement work into a precise programming system. That meant creating a shared nomenclature for movement patterns: joint angles, planes of motion, hand and foot placement, movement direction, force production, and loading.

The coach development vision was similar: turn a high-touch apprenticeship model into a curriculum that could train new coaches consistently.

## What I Built

On the programming side, I helped develop four flagship fitness products with roadmaps, progression logic, and precision movement prescriptions. The movement coding system allowed coaches to reproduce complex exercises across different clients and goals.

On the coach development side, I built and refined onboarding, training curriculum, and coach evaluation processes. I trained 11+ new coaches and used structured feedback, personality tools, and teaching systems to improve communication and coaching quality.

I also worked on sales and partnerships:

- Formalized Gymnazo's sales approach, including talk tracks, KPI tracking, and the first version of a franchise sales playbook.
- Helped initiate and manage a strategic partnership with MINDBODY.
- Created feedback loops between Gymnazo staff and the MINDBODY product team.
- Built systems across sales, programming, and coaching so the company could scale beyond founder-led delivery.

## Execution

The work required translating tacit coaching expertise into repeatable systems. A great coach might know intuitively how to regress a movement for a 71-year-old snowboarder or progress it for an athlete. The product challenge was making that judgment teachable.

We tested programming approaches, refined movement nomenclature, trained coaches against shared standards, and continuously adjusted based on client outcomes and coach performance.

## Impact

The work helped Gymnazo grow substantially, including 209% year-over-year revenue growth tied to the launch and refinement of flagship fitness products.

The coach development program evolved into a standalone certification product sold online for roughly $2,000. Gymnazo also expanded its ability to deliver end-to-end wellness programs for corporate companies with 1,000+ employees, integrating movement, nutrition, and rehabilitation programming.

## Lessons

The core lesson was that expert judgment scales only when you name it. Once the team had a shared language for movement, progression, coaching, and sales, the business could train people faster and deliver more consistent outcomes.

Precision did not make the product colder. It made the coaching more adaptable, because everyone had a clearer map of what to change and why.
