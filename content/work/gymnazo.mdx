## What Was Built

Gymnazo had a valuable but hard-to-scale asset: expert movement coaching that worked because experienced coaches could see, cue, and progress people in ways most fitness businesses could not.

I helped turn that expertise into repeatable systems: flagship programming, precision movement language, coach onboarding, certification curriculum, sales playbooks, partnership workflows, and feedback loops between client delivery and operations.

The work helped turn expert-led movement coaching into a system other coaches could learn, reproduce, and improve.

## My Role

I worked across product, programming, sales, partnerships, and coach development. I helped create fitness products, train coaches, formalize sales systems, initiate partnership workflows, and connect field feedback back into product and operations.

On the coaching side, I helped train 11+ new coaches and turn tacit movement expertise into curriculum, standards, and evaluation loops. On the business side, I helped connect the product, coaching, and sales system so the company could scale without relying only on founder-level delivery.

## How We Measured Success

The whole bet was that expert coaching could be reproduced by people who were not the founders. That set the bar:

- **A new coach had to deliver the product.** If quality depended on the founders being in the room, nothing had actually been systematized.
- **The curriculum had to stand on its own.** The real test of whether tacit expertise had been captured was whether someone would pay for it separately from the gym.
- **Demand had to be durable enough to justify capacity.** Growth that does not support adding a location is a spike, not a system.
- **Precision could not cost warmth.** Standardizing movement language and progression logic had to make the experience more consistent without making it feel scripted.

The work helped Gymnazo grow substantially, including 209% year-over-year revenue growth tied to the launch and refinement of flagship fitness products.

The coach development program evolved into a standalone certification product sold online for roughly $2,000. Gymnazo served 3,500+ customers, creating demand strong enough to justify a third location while expanding corporate wellness delivery.

## Why It Mattered

Expert judgment does not scale until it is named. Gymnazo had a high-quality service, but the product depended on making movement language, progression logic, coaching standards, and sales motion reproducible.

Precision made the business more scalable, not colder. It gave coaches a clearer map of what to change, customers a more consistent experience, and the business a way to grow without diluting the product.
