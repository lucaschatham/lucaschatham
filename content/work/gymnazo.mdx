## What Was Built

Repeatable systems for a high-skill fitness business: flagship programming, precision movement language, coach onboarding, certification curriculum, sales playbooks, partnership workflows, and feedback loops between client delivery and operations.

The work helped turn expert-led movement coaching into a system other coaches could learn, reproduce, and improve.

## My Role

I worked across product, programming, sales, partnerships, and coach development. I helped create fitness products, train coaches, formalize sales systems, initiate partnership workflows, and connect field feedback back into product and operations.

On the coaching side, I helped train 11+ new coaches and turn tacit movement expertise into curriculum, standards, and evaluation loops.

## Result

The work helped Gymnazo grow substantially, including 209% year-over-year revenue growth tied to the launch and refinement of flagship fitness products.

The coach development program evolved into a standalone certification product sold online for roughly $2,000. Gymnazo served 3,500+ customers, creating customer demand strong enough to justify a third location while expanding corporate wellness delivery.

## Why It Mattered

Expert judgment does not scale until it is named. Gymnazo had a high-quality service, but the product depended on making movement language, progression logic, coaching standards, and sales motion reproducible.

Precision made the business more scalable, not colder. It gave coaches a clearer map of what to change and why.

## What This Proves

I can operationalize expert knowledge. Whether the domain is movement, health, or AI data operations, I know how to turn founder-led judgment into systems a team can use.
